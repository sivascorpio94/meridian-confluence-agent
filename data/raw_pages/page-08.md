# PaySure Access – Quick Start

> **Status:** Draft — contractor contribution, awaiting Payments Team review before publication.

Need PaySure access fast? Here's the quick path.

## Quick request

Just send an email to **payments-team@meridian.example** with your role and manager's name. The Payments Team Lead usually approves these same-day.

## Roles

- `PAYSURE_VIEWER` — read-only access to transaction history
- `PAYSURE_OPERATOR` — can process refunds and adjustments
- `PAYSURE_ADMIN` — full admin access to payment rules and reconciliation

Pick the one you need based on your job title and ask your manager if you're unsure.

## Timeline

Same-day approval is typical. You should have access within a few hours of approval.
