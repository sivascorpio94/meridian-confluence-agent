# Beacon Support Desk – How to Submit a Ticket

Beacon is Meridian's internal IT support ticketing system. This page explains how to create and track support requests.

## How to submit a ticket

### Online (preferred)

1. Go to **Beacon Support Portal** (https://beacon.meridian.example)
2. Log in with your Meridian credentials
3. Click **"Create New Ticket"**
4. Fill in:
   - **Category**: Hardware, Software, Network, VPN, Account access, Other
   - **Subject**: Brief description of your issue
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation, steps you've tried, error messages
   - **Urgency**: Low, Medium, High, Critical
   - **Attachments**: Screenshots, logs, etc. (optional)
5. Submit — you'll get a ticket number via email

### By email (for urgent issues only)

Send an email to **support@beacon.meridian.example** with:

- Subject: "[URGENT]" followed by a one-line description
- Body: Detailed explanation of your issue and what you've tried

Email submissions are lower priority; the Portal is faster.

## Ticket status and SLA

| Urgency | Response time | Resolution SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 24 hours | 5 business days |
| Medium | 4 hours | 2 business days |
| High | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| Critical | 15 minutes | 4 hours |

Response time is the first human reply; resolution is when the issue is fixed or closed.

## Tracking your ticket

1. Log into the **Beacon Support Portal**
2. Go to **"My Tickets"** to see all your open and closed requests
3. Click any ticket to view status, comments, and history
4. You can reply to your ticket directly from the portal

You'll also receive email notifications when your ticket is updated.

## Tips for faster resolution

- Be specific: "can't login" is less helpful than "getting 'SSL certificate invalid' when connecting to vpn.meridian.example"
- Provide error messages and screenshots
- Include relevant system info (OS, browser version, IP address if applicable)
- Try basic troubleshooting first (restart, clear cache, update browser)

## Escalation

If your ticket is not resolved within the SLA:

1. Reply to your ticket and mention **@escalation**
2. Explain why the resolution is unsatisfactory
3. A Beacon manager will review within 2 hours

## Out-of-scope requests

Beacon does not handle:

- Software development or coding questions (ask your team lead or the eng-help Slack channel)
- Business process or policy questions (contact HR or your manager)
- Third-party SaaS support (contact the vendor directly)

For these, you'll be directed to the appropriate team.
