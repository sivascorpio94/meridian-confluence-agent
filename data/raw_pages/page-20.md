# IT Asset Request (Laptop/Monitor)

This page explains how to request IT hardware (laptops, monitors, keyboards, docking stations, etc.) through the HR-IT department.

## How to request hardware

1. Open the ServiceNow Asset Catalog and search for **"IT Hardware Request"**
2. Select the hardware category and specific item you need
3. Fill in:
   - **Justification**: Why you need the hardware (new hire, replacement, upgrade, etc.)
   - **Shipping address**: Your office or home (if remote)
   - **Urgency**: Standard (5–7 business days) or Expedited (2–3 business days)
4. Submit — HR-IT will review and fulfill your request

**SLA:** 5–7 business days for standard orders; 2–3 for expedited

## Available hardware

Common items available through the catalog:

- **Laptops**: MacBook Pro 16" (2024), Dell XPS 15, Lenovo ThinkPad X1
- **Monitors**: Dell UltraSharp 27" (4K), LG UltraWide 34"
- **Accessories**: Mechanical keyboards, vertical mice, laptop stands, monitor arms, docking stations

Custom requests (gaming laptops, high-end workstations) require manager approval and IT review.

## Shipping and setup

- Hardware ships to your address directly from the vendor
- You are responsible for unpacking and initial setup
- If setup assistance is needed, HR-IT can schedule a 30-minute onboarding call
- Return of old hardware is handled via a prepaid shipping label (included in your package)

## Hardware lifecycle

- All hardware is tracked in Meridian's IT asset management system
- You are responsible for the equipment assigned to you
- If hardware is lost, stolen, or damaged, report it to HR-IT immediately
- Damaged equipment may be your responsibility if the damage is due to negligence (not covered by accident insurance)

## Questions

For hardware requests, reach out to HR-IT at **hr-it@meridian.example** or stop by the HR-IT office on the 3rd floor (Building A).
