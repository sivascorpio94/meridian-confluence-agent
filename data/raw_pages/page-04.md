# New Hire Onboarding Checklist – Engineering

Complete these items during your first two weeks. Check off each item with your manager during your week-2 1:1.

## Week 1

- [ ] Laptop and monitor delivered (see IT Asset Request if anything is missing)
- [ ] VPN and remote access configured
- [ ] Added to team Slack channels and calendar
- [ ] GitHub/GitLab access confirmed with your team lead

## Week 1–2: system access

Request these based on your team's assignment. Not everyone needs everything on this list — check with your manager first.

- [ ] **Mosaic UI**: request `MOSAIC_UI_READONLY` access via the ServiceNow catalog if your team supports the advisory console
- [ ] **Orion Gateway**: request API consumer access if you'll be integrating with internal services
- [ ] **Vault namespace**: request a namespace for your team if you don't already have one to store secrets in

## Week 2

- [ ] Shadow an on-call engineer for at least one incident review
- [ ] Complete the internal security awareness training module
- [ ] Confirm you can access the team's runbook space

## Questions

Reach out to your manager first for anything role-specific. For general access questions, refer to the Meridian Platform Access FAQ or contact your manager.
