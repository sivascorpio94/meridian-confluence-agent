# Northstar CRM Access Request (Sales)

This procedure applies to Sales team members requesting Northstar CRM access.

## Prerequisites

- You must be a full-time Meridian employee (contractors can request through their agency)
- Your manager must confirm that your role requires CRM access

## How to request access

1. Open the ServiceNow Access Catalog and search for **"Northstar CRM Access — Sales"**.
2. Select the role you need (typically `NORTHSTAR_REP` for individual contributors, `NORTHSTAR_MANAGER` for team leads).
3. Provide your team assignment and a brief justification (e.g. "new hire, need to manage accounts for West region").
4. Your request routes to the **Sales Ops Manager** for approval.
5. Upon approval, access is provisioned automatically.

**SLA:** 1 business day

## After access is granted

- You can log in to Northstar immediately after approval
- Your team lead will schedule a brief orientation on account assignment and pipeline expectations
- All activity in Northstar is tracked and visible to your manager
- Inactive accounts are reviewed monthly; contact your manager if you're unsure about account ownership

## Revoke access

If you change teams or leave the Sales organization:

1. Your manager submits a revocation request through ServiceNow
2. Your access is revoked immediately
3. Your open opportunities are reassigned by the Sales Ops Manager

## Questions

Contact your team lead first. For technical issues, reach out in `#northstar-support` on Slack or email **northstar-support@meridian.example**.
