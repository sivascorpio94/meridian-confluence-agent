# Mosaic UI Access Guide

Mosaic UI is the console used to look up client account details and transaction history. If you need access, here's how to get it.

## Requesting access

Most people just need read access, which is the `mosaic-ui-read-only` role. To request it:

1. Send an email to **helpdesk@meridian.example** with the subject line "Mosaic UI Access Request."
2. Include your manager's name and a short reason for the request (e.g. "new hire, need to look up account records").
3. The helpdesk forwards your request to the **IT Manager**, who approves standard read-only requests.
4. Once approved, IT will set up your access and email you a confirmation.

Turnaround is usually about a week, depending on how backed up the helpdesk queue is. If you haven't heard back after 10 business days, reply to your original email and CC your manager.

## Admin access

If you need to make changes in Mosaic UI rather than just view records, mention that in your email and the IT Manager will loop in the Platform team to sort out the right permissions.
