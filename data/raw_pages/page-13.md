# Northstar CRM Access Request (Support)

This procedure applies to Support team members requesting Northstar CRM access to view customer accounts and log support cases.

## Prerequisites

- You must be a member of the Support organization (full-time employee or contractor via Beacon Support Desk)
- Your team lead must confirm that your role requires CRM access

## How to request access

1. Open the ServiceNow Access Catalog and search for **"Northstar CRM Access — Support"**.
2. Select the `NORTHSTAR_SUPPORT_AGENT` role (this is the standard role for Support).
3. Provide your team assignment and start date, along with a brief justification (e.g. "new support agent, need CRM access to assist customers").
4. Your request routes to the **Support Manager** for approval.
5. Upon approval, access is provisioned automatically.

**SLA:** 1 business day

## What you can do with Northstar

As a Support Agent, you can:

- View customer account details and contact information
- See active opportunities and related sales interactions
- Log support cases and link them to customer accounts
- Add notes to customer records
- View the history of past support interactions

You cannot:

- Edit customer accounts or opportunity details
- Create new opportunities
- Access internal sales forecasts or pipeline data
- Change customer status or other administrative fields

## Getting started

Once you have access:

1. Log in to Northstar with your Meridian credentials
2. Search for customers by account name, customer ID, or email
3. Use the "Case Log" tab to record support interactions
4. Link your case to an open opportunity if the customer is in an active sales conversation

Your team lead will provide account-specific training during your first week.

## Questions

Contact your Support Manager or the Support Desk. For technical issues, reach out in `#northstar-support` on Slack or email **northstar-support@meridian.example**.
